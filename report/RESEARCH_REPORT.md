# Part 1: Customer Research Report
## Man Matters — Stage 1 Hairfall Kit

---

## Brand Selected
**Man Matters** — a Mosaic Wellness brand focused on men's health and grooming, specifically their hair care product line.

**Product Studied**: Stage 1 Hairfall Kit

---

## Customer Conversations

### Customer 1
- **Product Used**: Stage 1 Hairfall Kit
- **Why They Purchased**: A friend with similar thinning suggested they try it
- **What They Liked**: The gummies are simple to take every day, and shedding has reduced by month 4
- **What They Disliked**: Skipped the gummies on hectic days — broke the daily habit
- **What They Want Improved**: Daily nudges via the app or WhatsApp reminders
- **Key Insight**: Even when a product works, inconsistent usage undermines results. The customer needed external accountability.

### Customer 2
- **Product Used**: Stage 1 Hairfall Kit
- **Why They Purchased**: At 24, kept finding more hair on their pillow and wanted to start somewhere without prescription drugs
- **What They Liked**: Hair fall eased after roughly 10 weeks, and the gummies actually taste nice
- **What They Disliked**: Saw no visible change for the first 5 weeks and almost gave up entirely
- **What They Want Improved**: A progress tracker that shows whether it's working before day 90
- **Key Insight**: The 5–10 week "dead zone" where nothing visible happens is a critical dropout risk. Customers need intermediate signals of progress.

### Customer 3
- **Product Used**: Stage 1 Hairfall Kit
- **Why They Purchased**: Baldness runs in the family, so they wanted to get ahead of it early
- **What They Liked**: Noticeably fewer strands collecting in the shower drain
- **What They Disliked**: Shedding in week 3 was temporary but it really alarmed them
- **What They Want Improved**: An upfront heads-up that short-term shedding is normal and expected
- **Key Insight**: The temporary shedding phase is medically expected but emotionally devastating for uninformed customers. Proactive communication could prevent panic.

### Customer 4
- **Product Used**: Stage 1 Hairfall Kit
- **Why They Purchased**: Didn't want to risk the side effects of Minoxidil or Finasteride
- **What They Liked**: Zero side effects, and hair looks slightly fuller after 3 months
- **What They Disliked**: Couldn't tell if they were Stage 1 or Stage 2
- **What They Want Improved**: A clear stage check that explains why a particular kit is suggested
- **Key Insight**: Self-assessment of hair loss stage is difficult without guidance. Customers fear using the wrong product, which creates decision paralysis and anxiety.

### Customer 5
- **Product Used**: Stage 1 Hairfall Kit
- **Why They Purchased**: A stressful new job and erratic sleep were taking a toll on their hair
- **What They Liked**: Shedding has dropped and the shampoo has a pleasant scent
- **What They Disliked**: The refill took 12 days to arrive — supply ran out
- **What They Want Improved**: Quicker delivery along with proactive shipment updates
- **Key Insight**: When treatment depends on daily consistency, supply disruptions don't just inconvenience — they undermine the entire therapeutic outcome.

---

## Patterns Across Conversations

### Pattern 1: Journey Anxiety & Lack of Progress Visibility (Customers: C1, C2, C3, C4)
**4 out of 5 customers** experienced some form of anxiety about whether the treatment was working. This manifests as:
- Panic at temporary shedding (C3)
- Wanting to quit during the first 5 weeks of no visible change (C2)
- Uncertainty about being on the right stage/kit (C4)
- Breaking routine due to no visible reinforcement (C1)

### Pattern 2: Poor Treatment Adherence (Customers: C1, C2)
Customers struggle to maintain daily consistency, especially during busy periods. Without reminders or accountability, they skip doses, which delays results and creates a negative feedback loop.

### Pattern 3: Stage Confusion (Customer: C4)
Without a clear self-assessment tool, customers can't determine their hair loss stage and worry they're using the wrong product.

### Pattern 4: Supply Chain Disruption (Customer: C5)
Late refills break treatment consistency, undermining the daily adherence the product depends on for efficacy.

---

## Problems Considered

1. **Journey Anxiety & Progress Visibility** — Customers can't tell if treatment is working, leading to panic and dropout
2. **Adherence & Habit Formation** — Daily routine is hard to maintain without reminders
3. **Stage Assessment Confusion** — Customers struggle to self-identify their hair loss stage
4. **Supply Chain & Refill Management** — Late deliveries break treatment continuity

---

## Problem Chosen

**Journey Anxiety & Lack of Progress Visibility**

---

## Why This Problem Over Others

Journey anxiety is not just one problem — it's the **root amplifier** that makes every other problem worse:

- When customers **can't see progress**, they lose motivation to maintain adherence (amplifies Pattern 2)
- When customers **panic at shedding**, they quit treatment entirely (amplifies dropout)
- When customers **don't understand their stage**, they lose confidence in the product (amplifies Pattern 3)
- When **refills are late** and there's no journey context, customers see it as a reason to stop (amplifies Pattern 4)

Solving journey anxiety creates a **cascading positive effect**: informed, reassured, and progress-aware customers are more likely to stay consistent, less likely to panic at normal shedding, and more motivated to reorder on time.

Technology can directly address this through:
- Real-time progress tracking with weekly milestones
- Proactive, science-based education about treatment phases
- AI-powered instant guidance for questions and concerns
- Visual progress indicators that provide intermediate signals before full results appear

---

## Problem Statement

> **Customers of Man Matters' Stage 1 Hairfall Kit lack structured guidance, progress visibility, and real-time support during their 12–16 week treatment journey. This information gap creates anxiety during the normal shedding phase (weeks 2–4), destroys treatment adherence, and causes premature product abandonment — often just weeks before visible results would have appeared. The root cause is not product efficacy but a communication and experience gap between purchase and results.**
