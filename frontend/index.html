    Pattern Analysis

    #1

    Journey Anxiety & Lack of Progress Visibility

    4 out of 5 customers experienced anxiety about whether the treatment was working. The combination of temporary shedding, slow visible results, and no way to track progress created a dangerous "should I quit?" moment around weeks 3–5.

    Customers: C1, C2, C3, C4
    #2

    Poor Treatment Adherence

    Customers struggle to maintain daily consistency, especially during busy periods. Without reminders or accountability, they skip doses which delays results further.

    Customers: C1, C2
    #3

    Stage Confusion & Kit Mismatch Fear

    Customers can't self-assess their hair loss stage, leading to fear of using the wrong kit and getting suboptimal results.

    Customers: C4
    #4

    Supply Chain & Refill Disruption

    Late refills break treatment consistency, undermining the very adherence the product depends on.

    Customers: C5
